What it does

Built around how the Box actually works

Every mechanic below maps to something the bot genuinely tracks per server — not flavor text bolted onto a generic bot.

🐞

Pick your own hero name

Nobody is stuck being "Ladybug." Set your alias once when you first transform with a Miraculous — it's remembered from then on.

💥

Use Power, for real

Lucky Charm, Cataclysm, Venom, Akumatize, Sublimation, Gift — every one of the 19 signature moves has its own working action.

🔗

Unify

Hold two or more Miraculous at once and fuse them into a single transformation, gaining every power and tool together.

🛡️

A real Guardian role

One member distributes the Box — giving, reclaiming, and handing off Guardian duty — without touching anyone else's powers.

🦋

Akumatize is yours, not theirs

Whoever holds the Butterfly wields it. Same for the Peacock's Amok. Using a power never depends on being the Guardian.

🖱️

Buttons, not memorization

One slash command posts the panel. Every action after that — transform, unify, distribute — is a click, not a typed line.

Questions

Before you add it

Do members have to type any commands?

No. The only typed action in the entire bot is the one-time /panel command. Every action after that — transforming, using a power, distributing the Box — is a button or a dropdown.

Can one member hold more than one Miraculous?

Yes — this is required for Unify. The Guardian can give the same member several Miraculous, and their "My Miraculous" screen lists every one they're holding.

Who can use Akumatize or Amok?

Whoever is transformed with the Butterfly or Peacock Miraculous — not necessarily the Guardian. Distribution and power use are kept separate on purpose.

Does the bot save progress?

Yes. Who holds what, who's transformed, and every chosen hero name are all saved per server and survive a restart.
